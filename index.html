The vow

The bell will never tell you what you’ll see down there. No one can. We tested every signal the ocean offers — satellites, buoys, upwelling, temperature — and watched each one fail to call the water.

So the bell doesn’t promise. It positions. Flat, dry, glassy, lit, warm — every card that can be stacked, stacked. The water keeps the last card. It always has.

When the bell rings, that’s the day to gamble.
