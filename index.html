It rings about twenty-one mornings a year.

A machine watches the swell, the wind, the rain, the tide and the light over Laguna’s coves. Most days it keeps quiet. When every knowable thing lines up — the bell rings.

The honest part

Nobody can forecast what you’ll see down there — not this machine, not anyone. Water clarity changes in days and answers to no free data. We tested it. Thoroughly.

So the bell doesn’t promise. It positions you: flat, dry, sunny, warm, easy — every card that can be stacked, stacked. The water is the last unknown.

When the bell rings, that’s the day to gamble.
