Terms

Short, because the service is small and honest about it.

What this is

The Dive Bell reads public ocean data — swell, wind, rain, tide, cloud — and scores diving conditions at named sites. When a site’s conditions meet its published threshold, subscribers get a message. It is free, it is a personal project, and it sells nothing.

What it is not

The bell describes conditions. It does not certify safety. It cannot see rip currents, boat traffic, your gear, your training, your buddy, or your lungs. It has never been in the water.

Every dive decision is yours. Check the water with your own eyes before you enter it, and turn the dive whenever something feels wrong. The bell will still be there tomorrow.

Forecasts are wrong sometimes — ours included, and we publish how often. Use the bell as one input among many: your own look at the water, local knowledge, and your training always outrank it.
